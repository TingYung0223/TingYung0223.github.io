About

I am a master's student in the Computer-Aided Engineering Group, Department of Civil Engineering, National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Rih-Teng Wu. I work on deep reinforcement learning for autonomous aerial robots: policies that decide where a UAV should look, how it should move, and when its job is done.

My current project teaches a UAV to find, follow and finish cracks on 3D bridge piers with continuous control, an explicit surface memory, and validation on the ArduPilot flight stack. Next, I am taking this policy from simulation to real flight.

Interests: robot learning, sim-to-real transfer, active perception, aerial autonomy.
